# Research Notes on Machine Learning

## Neural Networks
Neural networks are computational models inspired by biological neural networks.
They consist of layers of interconnected nodes (neurons).

### Key Concepts
- **Backpropagation**: Algorithm for training neural networks
- **Gradient Descent**: Optimization method for minimizing loss
- **Activation Functions**: ReLU, Sigmoid, Tanh

## Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)
GNNs extend neural networks to work with graph-structured data.
They are used for:
1. Social network analysis
2. Molecular property prediction
3. Knowledge graph completion

## RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
RAG combines retrieval systems with generative models.
- **Vector RAG**: Uses embedding similarity for retrieval
- **Graph RAG**: Uses knowledge graph traversal for retrieval
- **Hybrid RAG**: Combines both approaches
