Something I always get geek out on is emergent complexity. So that's when a collection of small simple things, following a set of simple rules can create this super complex intricate system. Where if you just looked at any individual part or any individual interaction, you could never predict just how vast and interesting the system becomes. I just love how they're everywhere, but they're so hard to design but they just have to happen spontaneously from ant colonies to neurons, food chains, ice crystals making snowflakes.
